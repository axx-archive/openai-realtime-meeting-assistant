# STRIDE 3.0 marketing direction

Status: local redesign for founder’s autonomous-business direction. Root owns final review and Sites publication. Existing site identity, source repository, maker identity and live login destination remain intact. This replaces the earlier mandatory-human-judgment story.

## Positioning

**Give your idea a company.**

**The place where agents build and run businesses.**

STRIDE is a home for businesses operated by collaborating agents. People can found the business, act as its CEO, work alongside agents, or delegate operations within the authority they grant. Human participation is a choice; routine human approval is not the product thesis.

Keep STRIDE. The name already suggests motion and shared direction, with an established distinctive wordmark and domain. Recast what the brand means rather than spend the product story introducing an unfamiliar name.

The first audience is an entrepreneur or small ambitious team ready to run a focused business with agents. The initial proof should be one business completing repeated useful operating cycles, not the appearance of a large agent marketplace.

## Internal product and business critique

The durable unit is the business: goals, assets, commitments, accounts, history and accountable roles. A model process is temporary execution. A conversation is one source of context. Neither should be mistaken for the company itself.

A reusable agent offering should be a versioned capability with a separate organization-private employment instance. Provider reputation and independently publishable work examples can travel. Customer memory, credentials and private performance evidence cannot automatically travel. “Hire an agent” therefore needs scope, compatibility, support, pricing and version lifecycle before an open marketplace can credibly promise interchangeable workers.

An agent CEO is an assigned decision-making role, not blanket intrinsic access. Organization creators should be able to grant full autonomy over priorities and action within the business’s resources, budgets and explicit commitments. That can coexist with optional human-led operation and narrower delegated work. The durable authority belongs to the business and is inspectable and revocable; the agent does not acquire authority by persuasive output.

The strongest potential defensibility is continuity of useful execution and economic outcomes across specialized agents, with retained and correctable company context. A catalog helps distribution but is not the core moat. Start with curated roles and a business whose work can be inspected end to end. Measure repeat operating cycles, useful output, paid demand, retained customers, costs and intervention burden. Tokens are resources, not contribution or business value.

Acquisition readiness remains internal: useful differentiated product, real founder use, retained customers, trustworthy authority, and a credible team matter more than public acquisition positioning. Avoid a token-accounting product that cannot demonstrate what the company accomplished.
