# End-to-end UX/UI audit — 2026-08-27

Branch `design/ux-audit-wave`, cut from `main` @ `56afdc11` in an isolated worktree so it
stays clear of the in-progress Stride work in the shared tree.

Audited against **production with real data** (`thebonfire.xyz`, signed in as AJ) because
empty states hide the truncation, line-break and density failures this pass was looking for.
Fixes were made and verified on a local build; each fix was also replayed against production
client-side (injected CSS only — no server change, no data written) to prove the before/after
on real content.

Surfaces covered: shell + primary rail, Home, Chat (threads, message list, composer, hover
actions), Files/Drive, Presentations studio, the native deck editor (toolbar, canvas, slide
rail, inspector Design/Notes/Scout tabs), Meet lobby / green room, account menu.
Widths: 360, 375, 390, 640, 641, 861, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920. Both themes.

Method note: **screenshots in the harness browser pane trail the DOM.** Three "findings"
died on inspection because of it (a blank Home, tabs appearing one step behind, missing
slide numbers). State was verified with JS measurement; screenshots were used only for
visual judgement.

### Findings that were investigated and rejected

Recorded because each one looked real and each one would have been a bad change:

- **Blank Home after SPA nav** — screenshot lag; elements measured visible at `opacity:1`.
- **Deck inspector tabs one step behind** — `aria-selected`, roving `tabindex` and panel
  `hidden` were all correct.
- **Slide thumbnails missing number badges** — every thumb has a `.deck-editor__thumb-num`.
- **Mobile dock occluding the last row of Drive cards.** The screenshot plainly shows the
  fixed dock over card text, and a first probe reported the scroller at `padding-bottom: 0`
  with a scroll gap of 0. Both were wrong: `.memory-tool` does not scroll (`scrollHeight ==
  clientHeight`), the **document** scrolls, and `.workspace` **already reserves
  `padding-bottom: 112px`** — exactly the dock's 56px box plus its 16px inset. The screenshot
  had simply caught content passing under a floating dock mid-scroll, which is correct
  behaviour. A padding fix was written, measured, found to be a no-op layered on existing
  padding (it would have produced a 168px dead gap), and reverted.
- **Ember rail icons as an accessibility failure** — they measure 4.12:1, above the 3:1 bar
  for graphical objects. Downgraded to a hierarchy opinion (below).

## Fixed on this branch

All six are correctness defects with measured before/after. None are taste calls.

### 1. Chat hover actions rendered up to 12,589px away from their message — CRITICAL

`.desktop-chat-interactions[data-has-reactions="false"]` is `position:absolute` with
`top:-18px; right:-8px` — "just above and just outside my bubble". But nothing on the
path was positioned: not `.scout-chat-msg__stack`, not `.scout-chat-msg`, not
`#scoutChatThread`. The containing block resolved all the way up to `#scoutPrivatePane`,
so **every** such bar stacked in the pane's corner.

Measured on production, 1440×900, four sample messages:

| | message top | bar top | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | −12544 | 45 | **12,589px** |
| before | −12413 | 45 | **12,458px** |
| before | −12147 | 45 | **12,192px** |
| before | −12014 | 45 | **12,059px** |
| after | −12544 | −12559 | **−15px** (correct) |

Every bar also sat at `right: 1448` on a 1440 viewport — 8px off-screen — which is why the
overflow "•••" button looked shaved. After: `right: 1413`, fully on screen.

**57 of 70 messages (81%) in the office thread have no reactions**, and this bar is the only
route to *add a first reaction, riff with Scout, reply in thread, or open more actions*.
Peer messages pinned left, user messages right; both wrong.

Fix: `position: relative` on `.scout-chat-msg__stack`.

### 2. Every semantic state hue was unreadable in light theme — 110 call sites

`--warn`, `--danger`, `--info`, `--live`/`--success` are the iOS system hues, tuned for a
black ground. The light theme is warm putty. As text they measured:

| token | on card #F2EDE4 | ground #DDD4C6 | well #CFC5B7 | deep well #C2B7A7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--warn` #FF9F0A | 1.76 | **1.40** | 1.21 | 1.04 |
| `--live` #30D158 | 1.73 | **1.38** | 1.19 | 1.02 |
| `--danger` #FF453A | 2.92 | **2.32** | 2.00 | 1.72 |
| `--info` #0A84FF | 3.13 | **2.49** | 2.14 | 1.85 |

AA needs 4.5. The worst instance is the one that matters most for a video product:
`#lobbyJoinError` — *"couldn't reach your camera or microphone — check permissions and try
again"* — rendered at **1.40:1 at 11px**. The only explanation a user gets for why they
cannot join a meeting was effectively invisible.

`--ember-text` already existed for exactly this reason; it had never been extended to the
other hues. Added `--warn-text` / `--danger-text` / `--info-text` / `--live-text` /
`--success-text` following the same recipe (hue and saturation held, lightness dropped until
AA clears on `--paper-200`, the deep well). Light values measure 7.6 / 6.0 / 5.2 / 4.5 across
the four surfaces. **Dark theme is untouched** — the dark tokens alias back to the raw hues,
which already measure 8.07 / 8.21 / 4.87 / 4.55 at worst on `#000`–`--surface-3`.

110 `color:` sites swapped with an anchored regex so `border-color:` and `background-color:`
were not touched (verified: zero collateral). Fills keep the bright hue — `--*-soft` chips,
dots and rings carry no text.

Two sites were deliberately reverted to the bright hue because they sit on dark surfaces even
in light theme: `.video-tile.is-muted .media-flag--muted` (on `--ink-900` #101013) and
`.greenroom__micbadge` (on the dark preview tile).

Verified live: `#lobbyJoinError` **1.40:1 → 6.04:1**.

### 3. 55% of every search bar was inert

`.memory-tool__search` is a 40px shell; the input inside it was 18px and unstretched,
leaving 11px dead bands top and bottom. Confirmed with a real dispatched click at
`shell.top + 4`: `document.elementFromPoint` returned the shell and `document.activeElement`
stayed `BODY` — the click did nothing.

Fix: `align-self: stretch` on the input. Verified: input 18px → **38px** in the 40px shell,
dead band 11px → **1px**.

### 4. Drive cards truncated their metadata on 8 of 9 cards

`.files-grid` used `minmax(210px, 1fr)`, packing 5 columns at 1440 and clipping
`.file-tile__meta` on every scout deliverable — overflow 30/44/30/30/30/51/51/59px, eating
the timestamp on all of them. The line promised three facts and delivered two, on every card.

Swept the track floor against the real 1113px container: 210px → 5 cols / 8 clipped;
224–264px → 4 cols / 1 clipped / 2px worst; 272px → 3 cols / 0 clipped. Chose **240px** —
4 columns, one 2px clip on the single longest string. 272 was rejected as over-correction
(losing two columns of density to recover 2px).

### 5. Studio project rows clipped their own standard status string by 4px

`.studio-project-row__meta` overflowed by exactly 4px on three rows — "Needs attention ·
Presentation", the app's own longest standard status+kind combination. A 4px cut reads as a
rendering defect rather than intentional truncation. Row `gap` 11px → 8px.
Verified: 3 clipped (worst 4px) → **0 clipped**.

### 6. Presenter notes were 66px shorter than the note

`.deck-editor__notes` had a fixed `min-height: 220px` while content measured
`scrollHeight 284` against `clientHeight 218` — cut mid-sentence, no scrollbar shown, in a
panel that had 694px to give. This is the field you read *while presenting*.
`min-height: clamp(220px, 40vh, 420px)`. Verified: 218px (66 short) → **358px (0 short)**.

## Surfaced, deliberately not changed

These are product, brand or content decisions. They need AJ's call, not a unilateral edit.

### Brand / hierarchy
- **Three of seven rail icons are ember at rest.** `.pd1-primary-nav__studio-wrap` and
  `.pd1-primary-nav__external` paint Presentations, Research and Content Studio at ~70%
  ember while Home/Meet/Chat/Files are graphite. They measure **4.12:1** on the rail, so this
  is *not* an accessibility failure — it is a hierarchy question. Colour is the strongest
  pre-attentive channel and here it is spent on a grouping that
  `.pd1-primary-nav__external-wrap::before` (a divider rule) already makes, which puts the
  secondary studios visually ahead of the primary destinations and spends ember's
  "live/attention" meaning inside the rail. Recommend dropping the at-rest tint and keeping
  ember for hover/active. Left alone because ember expression is a ratified brand call.

### Content quality (needs JS/content work, not CSS)
- **Raw tracking URLs used as descriptive text.** The Home screen's most prominent card
  reads `Bonfire Chat / https://x.com/CBSMornings/status/2092962883810259331?s=20`. Same in
  `.chat-thread-item__preview` (clipped 174px). A preview that is only a URL conveys nothing —
  should be the link's title or a domain chip.
- **Raw markdown leaking into reply chips.** `.desktop-chat-reply__text` renders
  `**Pupcasting:** video-first sports/pop-culture...` — asterisks and all, clipped by 727px.

### Naming / identity
- **Studio project titles are the raw prompt.** `.studio-project-row__title` is
  `nowrap`+ellipsis and overflows by 692/379/354/338/134/65px, so the list reads
  `Package Station Tenn, run thre…` above `Package Station Tenn, run two …` — two different
  projects, indistinguishable, because the cut lands before the differentiator. Two rows are
  literally *identical*: both titled "Turn this into a goal workflow:" (68% Needs you vs 72%
  Stopped). Needs derived titles, not a CSS fix.
- **Drive's page heading is "Home"**, colliding with the rail's top-level Home destination.

### Density / redundancy
- **Drive shows three competing "new" affordances at once**: sidebar `+ New`, header
  `New folder`, and a `+ New folder` chip — plus an "All files 9" chip directly above a
  "9 files" count, the same number twice, 24px apart.
- **Deck editor toolbar is 13 ungrouped equal-weight controls** — history, insert, navigate,
  zoom and output rendered as one flat run with uniform gaps and no separators.
- **Save is stated three ways at once**: "✓ Saved · Files" on the left, "Save a copy…" and an
  ember-filled "Save" on the right. The ember Save is the loudest pixel in the editor and it
  is loud permanently, including when there is nothing to save. Needs a decision on what
  Save *means* before restyling.
- **Insert tools are duplicated** — `Text / Upload / Rectangle / Ellipse` in the toolbar and
  again as a boxed 2×2 grid in the inspector ~700px away, in three visual treatments.
- **Progress contradicts status**: three studio rows show "100%" while labelled "Needs
  attention".
- **Inspector states the object count twice** ("17 objects · select one to refine" and
  "…one of the 17 already on this slide").

### Responsive
- **Deck toolbar wrap at ≤1024 puts authoring below export.** Row 1 becomes
  `Close · title · Saved · ‹1/10› · zoom · Download · Present · Save a copy… · Save`;
  row 2 becomes `Undo · Redo · Text · Upload · Rectangle · Ellipse`. The most-used tools land
  below the least-used, and it costs ~80px of canvas on an 800px-tall viewport. **Left alone
  because it is deliberate** — there is a comment stating the intent ("keep every command
  reachable without forcing the title, creation tools, and document actions into one clipped
  row"). Worth revisiting, but as a design decision.

### Touch targets (sub-44px, measured at 360/390/640)
`#topbarBell` 28×28 and `#topbarMobileAccount` 28×28 — the *only* two controls in the mobile
top bar. `#chatConvoBack` 30×30. `.scout-chat-msg__expand` ("Show more") 103×**14**.
`.lobby__more` 32×32. Drive chips and the grid/list toggle 26px tall.

### Meet lobby
The pre-join card is ~845×396 adrift in a 1440×900 field, with the camera preview — the one
thing you check before joining — at 414×266, about 8.5% of the screen. Device toggles are a
vertical stack of 36px circles to the *right* of the preview rather than on or under it,
which is where every comparable product puts them. The `join` button does not name the room
it joins. The permission-error copy sits *below* the button that retries it, so the remedy is
read before the cause.
