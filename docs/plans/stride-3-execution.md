## Product decision

STRIDE is where agents work together to build and operate businesses. A business has a mission, customers, resources, shared memory, specialist agents, and observed outcomes. Its essential loop is **choose → execute → observe → adapt**. Meetings, conversations, files, and tools contribute to that loop. People can own, participate, advise or delegate; mandatory human review is not the product thesis.

The founder explicitly selected this direction and authorized setting up STRIDE Builders as our first operating business, adding AJ for visibility, and using team conversations to build STRIDE. The latest request includes an exceptional website and freedom to rebrand. The lead retains STRIDE pending the marketing team's rendered work. Existing access should be used before asking for inputs; only concrete missing account ownership, credentials, or new spending commitments should reach AJ.

Business is a durable mission/policy/resource aggregate anchored to the existing organization and Project. The target operating kernel owns new Business Work; legacy Work retains its existing owner until an explicit migration. Agent service principals and standing mandates determine what agents may decide and execute, including external actions when explicitly granted. Agents cannot grant themselves additional authority or impersonate human reviewers. The decision-complete implementation contract is [stride-autonomous-business-contract.md](stride-autonomous-business-contract.md).

Home becomes the operating brief for a business. Work remains the durable home of exact outcomes; document/deck/research studios are tools within it. Company memory exposes sources, coverage, corrections and audience. Rich conversations and dependable calls connect agents and people. Web and Expo iOS share product grammar with layouts suited to their available space.

DISSENT supplies provider-independent routing and review behind STRIDE-owned context, budgets and tools. Actual provider/model/reasoning identity and independence remain visible. Experimental use is distinct from measured qualification. Token usage measures consumption; attributable results and observed customer outcomes establish contribution. Model agreement alone establishes neither correctness nor business value.

Business hypothesis: sell the ability to operate a business with an agent team, using a platform charge plus transparent execution budgets. Validate with a narrow operated-business proof before selecting final pricing. Public networks, marketplaces, licensing and white-label distribution remain options. No acquisition, investment or revenue outcome is promised. Private work stays private by default.

The differentiator to prove is successive business operation: a supervisor chooses useful work, specialists deliver it, policy admits decisions, real outcomes change the next episode, and every result remains inspectable across correction, revocation and restart. Human review is one optional policy input. Existing human acceptance events remain human opinions about exact versions; new agent evaluations use separate records.

The first proof is STRIDE Builders improving STRIDE onboarding across two episodes, using explicit observations to select the next improvement without step-by-step prompting. Current shipping work supplies the underlying evidence, memory, reliability and interface foundations; it is not yet a running autonomous business. The complete proof includes an unsuccessful result, a correction, a revoked source, restart recovery, costs, interventions and a feedback-withheld comparison. See the business contract for current primary-source market evidence and G0–G6 acceptance gates.
